# Art Bible — Black Orbit Outpost

## Direction lock (M2)

**Chosen path: A — Stylized illustrated 2D** (not pixel).

We keep **procedural / geometric placeholders** until commercial-friendly packs are integrated, but all new art must target:

- Clean silhouettes, readable at Deck resolution
- Painterly or vector-illustrated characters (not chunky pixel)
- Atmosphere first: fog, practical lights, blood as feedback

If pack cohesion fails later, we may still fall back to **B — crisp pixel**, but M2 locks intent to **A**.

## Typography

- Display: **Orbitron**
- Body / terminal: **Share Tech Mono**

## Entity read

- Player: small cyan operator with muzzle marker
- Core: larger teal beacon (always landmark)
- Trash: green / red / bile tones
- Chapter Elite: larger purple silhouette + dedicated HP bar + entrance flash

## Dialogue

PS2 talking-heads: bust portrait, nameplate, lower-third box. Faces can be stylized illustrations later; placeholders are geometric busts with emotion cues.
