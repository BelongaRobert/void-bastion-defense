## About this game (long)

**Black Orbit Outpost** is a premium act-based outpost defense game by **Belongarobert**.

You are a movable defender. The Outpost Core is your heart. The dark between the stars is not empty — and **Nyx** wants the can opened.

### Campaign

- **Act 1 — Dockyard Dark:** reclaim the yard. Face **The Dockmaster**.
- **Act 2 — Cold Storage:** freeze-burn corridors. Face **Cold Vault**.
- **Act 3 — Black Orbit:** hard vacuum plates. Face **Orbit Waker**.

Every act ends on **Wave 8: Chapter Elite + full supporting wave** — not a lonely arena boss.

Between waves: rest to heal and place hardpoints, shop salvage upgrades, and push deeper.

### Features that ship in 1.0

- Movable defender combat (WASD / gamepad)
- Two weapons (SMG + Shotgun), dash, Autogun Nest hardpoint
- Three full acts × 8 waves
- Meta unlock tree (Orbit Marks)
- Optional challenges (Fog, Double Runners, Core Bleed)
- Portrait dialogue story (protagonist vs Nyx)
- Local achievements (Steam achievements when App ID lands)
- Options: gore, screen shake, aim assist, dialogue speed, Deck UI scale
